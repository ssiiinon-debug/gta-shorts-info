Privacy Policy — Shorts Pipeline

Last updated: November 25, 2026

What the app does

Shorts Pipeline is a single-user automation tool. It uploads videos to the owner's own YouTube channels via the YouTube Data API and moves files within the owner's Google Drive via the Drive API.

Data access and storage

The app accesses only the Google accounts of its operator (YouTube channels and Drive folders). OAuth tokens are stored as encrypted repository secrets on GitHub and are never shared with third parties. No end-user data is collected, stored, sold, or shared. No analytics or tracking is used.

Data usage

Google account data is used solely to perform the actions described above, on behalf of and under the control of the account owner. The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
